Turn your own photos into a GTA 6-style multi-panel cover — Vice City sunset, neon glow, zero cost, fully offline. Built for the [make.fun gta6-cover-art-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).

## What it does

Upload a photo (or drag and drop it in) and Neon Vice Cover Studio arranges it into a bold, poster-ready cover. Pick a layout, pick a grade, tune the captions, export a crisp PNG — no account, no server, no upload of your photos anywhere. Everything runs in your browser.

## Layouts

Six multi-panel arrangements to choose from:

- **Hero Grid** — one big hero shot with supporting panels around it.
- **Comic Nine** — a nine-panel comic-book spread.
- **Tilted Snaps** — casually rotated snapshots, polaroid energy.
- **Full Bleed** — a single photo stretched edge to edge.
- **Split Duo** — two frames side by side, perfect for before/after or duo shots.
- **Story Strips** — tall vertical strips made for stories.

## Colour grades

Five Vice City-inspired grades, each with its own mood:

- **Vice Sunset** — warm orange-to-pink dusk.
- **Neon Nights** — electric pink and cyan after dark.
- **Golden Hour** — soft late-afternoon gold.
- **Miami Teal** — cool turquoise water and chrome.
- **Noir Drive** — high-contrast black-and-white night.

Layer on retro sun, skyline silhouettes, palm trees, halftone dots, film grain, and a vignette to finish the look.

## Photos and captions

- Upload or drag and drop your own photos into any panel.
- Pan and zoom each panel independently until the framing is right.
- Captions use a condensed-black title with a sunset gradient and neon glow, plus an italic subtitle.
- Seven caption presets for instant vibes — or type your own title and subtitle.

## Export

Export your cover as PNG in three sizes:

- `1080 × 1080` — square, for feeds.
- `1080 × 1350` — portrait, for feeds and posters.
- `1920 × 1080` — widescreen, for banners and wallpapers.

## Extras

- **Undo / redo** — every change is reversible.
- **Surprise me** — one click randomises layout, grade, and caption for instant inspiration.
- **Offline** — the whole studio works without an internet connection once loaded.

## Tips for a great cover

- Use bright, high-contrast photos — sunset shots and city lights grade best.
- Put your subject in the hero panel and let the smaller panels tell the rest of the story.
- Keep titles short (two to four words) so the condensed-black type stays huge and readable.
- Try Vice Sunset for golden-hour photos and Neon Nights for anything shot after dark.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the printed local address in your browser. No build step, no dependencies to install, no Rockstar logo, artwork, or fonts anywhere in the project — the title font is Noto Sans Condensed Black, released under the Open Font License (`app/fonts/OFL.txt`).
